Lesson Date: 2026-10-03
Lesson Title: Mary | Talking About Yourself
Short Introduction: Express pride, reflect on yourself, and choose the right words for difficult experiences.
Today's Focus: Talk about who you are now. Distinguish sacrifice from suffer, and pain from painful.
Topics: self-reflection, feelings, word forms

## Useful Phrase
English: I was proud of myself.
Japanese: 自分を誇りに思いました。
Explanation: Use be proud of someone. To describe the occasion, add when.
Example: I was proud of myself when I finished my speech.

## Useful Phrase
English: It doesn't resonate with me.
Japanese: 私には心に響きません／あまり共感できません。
Explanation: Use resonate with someone when an idea feels personally meaningful.
Example: I understand the message, but it doesn't really resonate with me.

## Natural English Upgrade
Original: I love my current self the most.
Natural: I like who I am now more than ever.
Japanese: 今の自分が今までで一番好きです。
Explanation: The original is grammatical. This version makes the comparison with your past self clearer.

## Japanese to English
English: I need some time to reflect on who I am.
Japanese: 自分と向き合って考える時間が必要です。
Explanation: Reflect on means think carefully about something.
Example: I care about the people around me.

## Comparison
Word A: sacrifice
Word B: suffer
Japanese: sacrifice＝犠牲／犠牲にする。suffer＝苦しむ。犠牲にすることと、苦しむことは同じではありません。
Explanation: Sacrifice is a noun and a verb. Suffer is a verb; suffering is a noun. The adjective sacrificial means relating to or involving sacrifice.
Examples:
It was a big sacrifice.
I sacrificed my free time for my family.
He suffered a lot.

## Comparison
Word A: pain
Word B: painful
Japanese: pain＝痛み・苦しみ（名詞）。painful＝痛い・つらい（形容詞）。
Explanation: Use pain, not painness. Painfulness exists, but pain is the usual choice here.
Examples:
I felt a lot of pain.
It was a painful experience.

## Pronunciation
English: sacrifice
IPA: US /ˈsæk.rə.faɪs/ | UK /ˈsæk.rɪ.faɪs/
Japanese: 最初の音節を強く。名詞も動詞も同じ発音です。
Explanation: Stress SAC. The last sound is /s/, not /z/.

## Quick Practice

### Multiple Choice
Question: Which sentence means someone gave something up for another person?
A: She suffered a lot.
B: She sacrificed her free time for her family.
C: She was proud of her family.
Answer: B
Explanation: Sacrifice something for someone means give something up for that person.

### Fill in the Blank
Question: Complete with one preposition: I was proud ___ myself.
Answer: of
Explanation: The pattern is be proud of someone.

### Error Correction
Question: Replace the incorrect word: I felt a lot of painness.
Answer: I felt a lot of pain.
Explanation: Pain is the noun. Painful is the adjective.

### Sentence Reorder
Question: Put the words in order. Start with It.
Items:
- with
- me.
- It
- resonate
- doesn't
Answer: It doesn't resonate with me.
Explanation: Use resonate with, not resonate to.

### Japanese to English Practice
Question: 「今の自分が今までで一番好きです」を英語で。Use more than ever.
Answer: I like who I am now more than ever.
Accepted answers:
I love who I am now more than ever.
Explanation: More than ever means more than at any time before.

### Short Answer
Question: Describe one small moment when you felt proud of yourself. One sentence is enough. You may invent an example.
Hint: I was proud of myself when...
Answer: I was proud of myself when I finished my speech.
Explanation: Your answer can be different. Use proud of and when to explain the moment.

### Self Check
Question: Can you make one sentence with sacrifice and one with painful?
Answer: I sacrificed my free time for my family. It was a painful experience.
Explanation: Sacrifice can be a verb. Painful describes an experience.
